Every custom carries a little story. Open a card to discover the moments that make a Bengali wedding so special.

01 আইবুড়ো ভাতAiburo Bhaat +

A loving farewell to single life, shared over a special meal with family on the eve of the wedding.

02 গায়ে হলুদGaye Holud +

Turmeric, laughter and blessings come together in a joyful celebration with loved ones.

03 শুভ দৃষ্টিShubho Drishti +

The bride and groom share their first auspicious glance, as family and friends celebrate around them.

04 মালা বদলMala Badal +

With smiles and playful cheers, the couple exchange flower garlands to welcome their life together.

05 সিঁদুর দানSindoor Daan +

A tender wedding blessing, when vermilion is placed in the bride’s hair parting to mark the sacred bond.
